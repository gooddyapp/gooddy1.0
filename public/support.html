Support

On this device

Logs and your profile stay on the device. If you created an account, a private copy is also stored for that sign-in. This page cannot reset that account.

Delete your account

Open the app, go to Settings, then Privacy and data, then Delete my account and all data. That removes the on-device copy and the signed-in account record. You can also email us and ask for the account copy to be deleted.

Contact
