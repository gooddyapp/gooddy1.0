Privacy policy

This policy covers the gooddy iPhone app, the gooddy Android app, and the gooddy website pages we publish, including this page. The apps are published as com.gooddy.app. The controller is gooddy. Contact: gooddy.wellness@gmail.com.

Age

gooddy is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children. If you are under 16, do not create a profile. If we learn we have an account for someone under 16, we will delete it.

What gooddy is

gooddy is a wellness companion for cycle notes, food, movement, habits, and a journal. Cycle dates, phase notes, calorie targets, and fertile-window estimates are educational estimates. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, contraception, or proof of ovulation.

Data you give us

Depending on what you choose to enter, gooddy holds your name, email, password (handled by the account provider, not stored in the app’s own notes), gender, birthday and the age calculated from it, height, weight, activity, diet, and goals. It can also hold cycle dates, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, appetite, meals, water, caffeine, steps, workouts, habits, and journal text. You do not have to fill in every field.

Cycle, symptom, and other body notes are health information. We ask for a separate health-data consent before we use them to personalize the app. Marketing email is a different, optional choice, and it is off unless you check that box. This version does not send marketing email.

Where it lives

The app keeps a copy on your phone or in this browser. That copy stays there until you delete it, delete the app, or clear the site data.

If you create an account, a second copy of your profile and logs is stored so you can sign in again. That copy is in a private database row (Supabase) that your signed-in user can read. It is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell it. Supabase acts as our processor. Its servers may be outside your country, including in the United States. If you never create an account, that copy is not made.

Ads

The iPhone and Android apps can show one banner from Google AdMob. We request a non-personalized ad. We do not send your cycle, symptoms, birthday, weight, journal, or email to Google to choose the ad, and we do not use health information for advertising or for tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Google may still receive device and network information, such as an IP address and device model, to deliver, secure, and measure that banner. On iPhone we do not ask you to allow tracking. The website pages for this policy do not run that banner. You can use gooddy without an account.

Other services

The app may request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request does not include your cycle or email. If you turn reminders on, Apple or Google delivers the notification you asked for. You can turn each reminder off in Settings. The system permission is requested only after you opt in.

A website host may keep ordinary connection logs, such as an IP address and the page requested, to run and protect the site. We do not add our own advertising cookies on these policy pages.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share health information with data brokers. We do not use health information to target ads. We do not claim gooddy is a medical device.

Why we use it

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use account and log data to provide the service you asked for (contract). We use health data only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by deleting the data or the account. We use the limited device data needed to show a non-personalized ad on the basis of providing the free app, and you can use the rest of the product if an ad does not load. We keep the service secure as a legitimate interest.

How long we keep it

We keep the on-device copy until you delete it. We keep the account copy until you delete the account. Deletion is meant to be complete. A backup at the database host may remain for a short time and then drop out of the backup cycle.

Your choices and rights

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy and data, then Delete my account and all data. That removes the on-device copy and, if you are signed in, the account record. This is the in-app account deletion path.

You can also email gooddy.wellness@gmail.com to access, correct, export, or delete the account copy, or to withdraw health-data consent. We may need to confirm the email on the account. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top. Continued use of gooddy after that date means the new policy applies. If we ever need fresh consent, we will ask for it.
